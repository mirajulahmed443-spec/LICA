01 — About

THE LICA
EXPERIENCE

“More than a café, LICA is a place where food, conversations and the magic of cinema come together.”

LICA brings together the relaxed atmosphere of a café with a visual identity inspired by the world of cinema. From its film-inspired décor to its warm lighting and casual dining atmosphere, every detail contributes to an experience designed around food, people and moments.

A cinema themed cafe in Guwahati for slow breakfasts, quick lunches and long evening conversations — a cafe in Beltola made for people who like their coffee with a little bit of film.

02 — The Story

THE STORY
BEHIND LICA

LICA — Light Camera Action Cafe — is a café, a kitchen and a little bit of a film set. Film cameras on the wall, wooden shelves, hanging greenery and warm amber pendant lamps set the frame; the food and the people complete the shot.

Whether you are dropping in for a grilled sandwich between classes, settling in with noodles and a conversation, or simply looking for a quiet corner in Beltola Tiniali, LICA keeps things simple: good food, warm lights and an atmosphere that feels like the interval of a good film.
